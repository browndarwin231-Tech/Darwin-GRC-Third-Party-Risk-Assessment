# Vendor Risk Assessment Evidence

## Purpose

This folder contains supporting evidence for the SecureCloud Solutions third-party risk assessment.

The evidence demonstrates how vendor security risks were identified, scored, documented, and prioritized.

## Evidence Included

- Vendor security questionnaire
- Vendor risk register
- Vendor gap assessment
- Vendor remediation plan
- Risk scoring results
- Conditional approval decision

## Assessment Areas

The vendor was evaluated across:

- Identity and Access Management
- Encryption
- Incident Response
- Vulnerability Management
- Penetration Testing
- Logging and Monitoring
- Backup and Recovery
- Security Awareness
- Third-Party Risk Management
- Business Continuity
- Data Protection

## Vendor Decision

**Conditionally Approved**

High-risk findings must be remediated or formally accepted before full vendor approval.
